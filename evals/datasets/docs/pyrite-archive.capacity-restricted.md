# pyrite-archive capacity allocation (restricted)

Restricted to the acme tenant. The figures in this document are commercially
sensitive and are not shared outside the tenant boundary.

## Allocation

The contracted capacity for pyrite-archive is 10000 messages per second, with
a committed p99 latency of 180 milliseconds. Overage is billed at a rate that
depends on the tenant agreement.

## Expansion

Capacity expansions require acme sign-off and are provisioned within one
business day of approval.
